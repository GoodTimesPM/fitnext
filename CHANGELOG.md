# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-21)

The first public release.

- Today, Train, Fuel, Kitchen and Progress, on a phone and on a wide screen
  with two or three panels side by side.
- 12 training programs, custom routines, guided workouts with a rest timer,
  history and personal records, over a catalog of 5,632 exercises (1,394
  animated).
- Food search across Open Food Facts, USDA, FatSecret and Edamam, with
  everyday units, barcode scan, and photo and voice logging.
- Recipes: 120+ starters, TheMealDB search, link import, and a grocery list.
- Optional sync between devices through Supabase, signed in by email link or code.
- Windows installer with auto-update from this repo's releases. The hosted web
  app installs to a phone's Home Screen.
